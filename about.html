Meet Dr. Fermin Morales

Founder and Medical Director of OxyLux Solutions.

MD, FACS

Four decades in medicine

Dr. Fermin Esteban Morales earned his medical degree in Havana, Cuba, in 1985 and has practiced medicine ever since. He completed general surgery residencies at Hospital General Docente “Dr. Carlos J. Finlay” in Havana and at Lehigh Valley Hospital in Allentown, Pennsylvania.

Before and during his U.S. training he spent nearly a decade as a surgical assistant at the Miami Heart Institute. He has taught surgery as an assistant professor at Penn State University and Florida International University, and today practices general and cosmetic surgery in Miami.

As Founder and Medical Director, Dr. Morales leads OxyLux’s clinical standards and treatment plans, bringing a surgeon’s eye for healing to every patient.
