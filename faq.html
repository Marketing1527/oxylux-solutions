Questions, answered

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Dr. Morales and our team speak Spanish.

Does hyperbaric oxygen therapy hurt?

No. The most common feeling is pressure in your ears as the chamber pressurizes, similar to a plane landing. Swallowing or yawning clears it, and we can pause at any time.

How long is a session?

Most sessions last 60 to 90 minutes, plus time to change and settle in. Plan on about two hours for your first visit.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your goals. Some clients come once to relax; others follow a series over several weeks. We’ll suggest a plan after your screening.

Who should not use HBOT?

People with certain lung conditions, an untreated collapsed lung, recent ear or sinus surgery, some medications, or who are pregnant may not be able to use the chamber. Our health screening covers this, and we may ask for your doctor’s clearance.

What should I wear?

We provide 100% cotton scrubs. Please skip lotions, perfume, hair products and makeup on session days, and remove jewelry and piercings where possible.

Can I bring my phone?

No. Phones and other electronics stay outside the chamber for safety. The chamber has an intercom so you can talk with your attendant at any time.

Do you take insurance?

Yes. OxyLux accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage for hyperbaric oxygen therapy depends on your plan and diagnosis, so call (305) 205-2738 before your first visit and we will help you check.

Send a booking request and we’ll call to go over your health screening and find a time that works.
