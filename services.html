Hyperbaric oxygen treatment

Private sessions in a hard-shell chamber, with a trained attendant every visit. Insurance accepted.

Our chamber

OxyLux uses an Oxycell OxyPro Genesis hard-shell chamber. You sit in a padded reclining seat with a wide window, soft lighting and an intercom to your attendant, who controls pressure and oxygen from the touchscreen outside.

  • Seated and recliningRoom to relax, read or nap. No lying flat in a tube.
  • Attended every sessionYour attendant watches the controls and checks on you throughout.
  • Cleaned between patientsSurfaces are disinfected after every session.

Your first session

A health screening, vitals check and your first 60–90 minute session. Plan on about two hours. We review your history and recommend how many sessions may help.

Treatment plans

Most protocols call for a series of sessions over several weeks. We schedule your series up front so you keep your preferred times.

Surgical and cosmetic recovery

Sessions before and after surgery or cosmetic procedures to support healing, swelling and bruising recovery, coordinated with your surgeon’s plan.

Wound care

HBOT is FDA-cleared for diabetic and other non-healing wounds, as well as tissue damage after radiation. Bring any records from your treating physician to your first visit.

Athletes and active adults

Sessions scheduled around training and competition to support recovery from strain and injury.

Insurance

OxyLux works with most major insurance plans. Coverage for HBOT depends on your plan and diagnosis.

Call (305) 205-2738 before your first visit and our front desk will help you understand your coverage. Bring your insurance card and photo ID to your appointment.
