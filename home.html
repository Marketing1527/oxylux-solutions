What is hyperbaric oxygen therapy?

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) means breathing high-concentration oxygen inside a chamber pressurized above normal air pressure. The added pressure lets your blood carry more oxygen than it can at sea level.

Clients come to us as part of recovery after training, alongside a care plan from their doctor, or simply to rest and reset. Sessions typically last 60 to 90 minutes.
