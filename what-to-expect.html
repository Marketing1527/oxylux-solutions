What to expect

Here’s how a session goes from the moment you arrive.

  1. Reserve and sign online

    Choose your time with Reserve now and sign your consent form in the same step.

  2. Arrive 15 minutes early

    Bring your photo ID and insurance card. We review your health history, take your vitals and answer any questions.

  3. Change into cotton clothing

    We provide 100% cotton scrubs. Lotions, perfume, makeup, electronics and jewelry stay outside the chamber.

  4. Pressurization

    Pressure rises slowly over several minutes. Your ears will feel full, like descending in a plane. Swallowing or yawning clears them, and we go at your pace.

  5. Rest and breathe

    For most of the session you simply relax, nap or listen to music. Your attendant can hear you over the intercom the whole time.

  6. Depressurization and after

    Pressure comes down gradually. Most people feel relaxed afterward and can go straight back to their day.

Safety first

HBOT isn’t right for everyone. Certain lung conditions, recent ear surgery, some medications and pregnancy may mean you need clearance from your doctor or shouldn’t use the chamber at all.

That’s why every new client completes a health screening before their first session. If something needs a closer look, we’ll ask you to check with your physician first.
