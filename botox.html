What it is

Botox Cosmetic (onabotulinumtoxinA) is a prescription medicine that temporarily relaxes the muscles that cause certain facial lines. It is FDA-approved for frown lines, crow’s feet, forehead lines and platysma bands in adults.

A licensed medical professional examines you first and decides whether it is right for you. Results usually appear within days and last a few months.

Important safety information: The effects of botulinum toxin may spread from the injection site and cause serious symptoms hours to weeks later, including muscle weakness, double vision, drooping eyelids, and trouble speaking, swallowing or breathing, which can be life-threatening. Get medical help right away if these occur. Botox Cosmetic is a prescription medicine; your provider will review the full safety information with you.

What to expect

  1. Consultation

    We talk through your goals, health history and any prior injections.

  2. Treatment

    Your provider uses a very fine needle to make small injections. It usually takes about 30 minutes.

  3. Afterward

    Follow your aftercare instructions. Small bumps, redness or bruising usually fade quickly.

  4. See your results

    Results develop over the next several days. We can plan your next visit when they begin to fade.

Before you book

You will complete a consent form for this service when you reserve. Tell us if any of these apply to you, so we can talk first.

  • Allergy to any botulinum toxin product
  • Infection at the injection site
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Neuromuscular conditions such as myasthenia gravis
