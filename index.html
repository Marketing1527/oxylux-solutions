What is hyperbaric oxygen therapy?

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy, or HBOT, means breathing concentrated oxygen inside a chamber pressurized above normal air pressure. The added pressure lets oxygen dissolve into your blood plasma, so more of it can reach tissue that is healing.

HBOT is FDA-cleared for specific conditions, including slow-healing wounds and radiation injury. Many patients also use it to support recovery after surgery or cosmetic procedures, alongside the care plan from their doctor. A session typically lasts 60 to 90 minutes.

What pressure and oxygen do

Your body heals with oxygen. HBOT raises how much of it your blood can carry.

  • More oxygen in your bloodUnder pressure, oxygen dissolves directly into plasma and travels to areas with poor circulation.
  • Support for tissue repairHigher oxygen levels help the cells that rebuild skin, collagen and blood vessels do their work.
  • Calmer inflammationResearch suggests repeated sessions can reduce swelling and inflammation after injury or surgery.
  • New blood vessel growthA series of sessions can encourage new small blood vessels in tissue that is slow to heal.

Does hyperbaric oxygen therapy hurt?

No. The most common feeling is pressure in your ears as the chamber pressurizes, like a plane landing. Swallowing or yawning clears it, and the attendant can slow down or pause at any time.

Do you take insurance?

Yes. OxyLux accepts most major insurance plans. Coverage depends on your plan and diagnosis, so call us and we will help you check before your first visit.

Who should wait before trying HBOT?

Some lung, ear and sinus conditions, a recent flight, certain medications and pregnancy may mean you need to wait or get your doctor’s clearance. Your consent form and screening cover this.
