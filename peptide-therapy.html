What it is

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signals in the body. In peptide therapy, a licensed provider reviews your health history and goals and, when appropriate, prescribes a peptide protocol, usually small injections you learn to give at home.

Some peptides are not FDA-approved for the uses they are prescribed for, and some are prepared by compounding pharmacies. Your provider explains your options, the evidence and the risks before you start. Results vary.

What to expect

  1. Consultation

    We review your health history, medications and goals with you.

  2. Lab work

    Your provider may order blood work before recommending a protocol.

  3. Your protocol

    If peptide therapy is right for you, we explain the dose and how to use and store it.

  4. Follow-up

    Regular check-ins let your provider track your progress and adjust your plan.
