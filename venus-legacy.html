What it is

Venus Legacy combines multi-polar radio frequency with pulsed electromagnetic fields to warm the skin and the tissue beneath it.

The manufacturer reports FDA clearance for moderate to severe facial wrinkles in women with Fitzpatrick skin types I to IV, and for the temporary reduction in the appearance of cellulite. Results vary, and a series of sessions is usually recommended.

What to expect

  1. Consultation

    We look at your goals and confirm the treatment is a good fit for your skin.

  2. Treatment

    Your provider glides the applicator over the area. You should feel comfortable warmth.

  3. Session length

    Most sessions last 30 to 45 minutes, depending on the area.

  4. Afterward

    Mild redness or warmth usually fades within hours, and most people return to their day.

Before you book

You will complete a consent form for this service when you reserve. Tell us if any of these apply to you, so we can talk first.

  • Pacemaker or implanted electronic device
  • Metal implants near the treatment area
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Active infection in the treatment area
