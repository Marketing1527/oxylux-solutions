What is hyperbaric oxygen therapy?

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy, or HBOT, means breathing concentrated oxygen inside a chamber pressurized above normal air pressure. The added pressure lets your body take in more oxygen than it can at sea level.

Clients come to OxyLux to support recovery after training or a procedure, alongside a care plan from their doctor, or simply to rest and reset. A session typically lasts 60 to 90 minutes.

Does hyperbaric oxygen therapy hurt?

No. The most common feeling is pressure in your ears as the chamber pressurizes, like a plane landing. Swallowing or yawning clears it, and we can pause at any time.

What should I wear?

We provide 100% cotton scrubs. Please skip lotions, perfume, hair products and makeup on session days, and leave jewelry and electronics outside the chamber.

Who should not use HBOT?

Some lung, ear and sinus conditions, recent air travel, certain medications and pregnancy may rule it out or require your doctor’s clearance. Your consent form and screening cover this.
