What it is

IV therapy delivers fluids, vitamins and minerals through a small catheter in your arm. Because it goes directly into a vein, it bypasses your digestive system.

A licensed clinician reviews your health history first and decides whether IV therapy is right for you and what your IV should contain.

What to expect

  1. Health review

    We go over your history, medications and allergies, and check your vitals.

  2. IV placement

    A clinician places a small catheter, usually in your arm. You may feel a quick pinch.

  3. Relax

    Sit back while your IV runs, usually 45 to 60 minutes. Tell us right away if anything feels off.

  4. Back to your day

    Most people go straight back to their day. Keep an eye on the IV site for any redness or swelling.

Before you book

You will complete a consent form for this service when you reserve. Tell us if any of these apply to you, so we can talk first.

  • Heart failure or serious heart disease
  • Kidney or liver disease
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Allergy to any vitamin or ingredient
