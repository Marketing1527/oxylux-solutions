What it is

Massage therapy works the muscles and soft tissue to ease tension and support relaxation. Your therapist adjusts pressure and focus areas to what you need that day.

It pairs well with other OxyLux services as part of a recovery routine. Ask us about combining a massage with another session.

What to expect

  1. Tell us your goals

    Share any sore areas, injuries or pressure preferences with your therapist.

  2. Settle in

    You stay properly draped the entire time, and you can ask to skip any area.

  3. Your massage

    A typical session lasts 60 minutes. Speak up any time the pressure needs to change.

  4. Afterward

    Drink water and take it easy. Mild soreness the next day is common.

Before you book

You will complete a consent form for this service when you reserve. Tell us if any of these apply to you, so we can talk first.

  • Fever or contagious illness
  • Blood clots or deep vein thrombosis
  • Recent surgery or injury
  • Skin infection or open wounds
